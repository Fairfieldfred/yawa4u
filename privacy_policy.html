Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Introduction

YAWA4U ("we," "our," or "us") is a training tracker for strength, running, cycling and swimming. This Privacy Policy explains what information the YAWA4U mobile application (the "App") accesses, how it is used, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

The short version: YAWA4U is local-first. Your workouts, training plans and any health data you choose to import are stored on your device. Nothing leaves your device unless you turn on a feature that needs it (cloud backup, community uploads, or Strava). We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never share health data with third parties.

Health and Fitness Data

If you choose to connect YAWA4U to Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iOS) from More → Integrations, the App requests read-only access to the following data types:

  • Exercise sessions / workouts — the sport type, start and end time, and duration of workouts recorded by other apps and devices (for example Peloton, Garmin or Samsung Health).
  • Distance — the distance covered during those workouts.
  • Heart rate and active energy burned (Apple Health only) — to show average and maximum heart rate and calories for an imported workout.

How we use it: this data is used only to import your completed workouts into your YAWA4U training log, so they appear on the Calendar, in workout details (pace, speed, distance), and in your training statistics (for example weekly distance and totals per sport). This saves you from entering workouts by hand and lets you compare what you planned with what you actually did.

What we do not do: YAWA4U does not write, modify or delete any data in Health Connect or Apple Health. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never used to determine credit-worthiness or insurance eligibility, and never shared with third parties. It is not sent to our analytics or crash-reporting services.

Where it is stored: imported workouts are stored in the App's local database on your device. If you turn on Cloud Backup (see below), they are included in your private, encrypted backup file, which only your account can access.

Your control: access is only requested after you tap Connect, and you can revoke it at any time in the Health Connect app (Android) or Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices (iOS). Revoking access stops future imports. Deleting an imported workout in the App, or uninstalling the App, removes it from your device.

YAWA4U's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Apple HealthKit is handled in accordance with Apple's App Store Review Guidelines.

Data You Enter

Training cycles, workouts, exercises, sets, weights and reps, notes, workout feedback, and optional body measurements (height, weight, body-fat percentage) that you enter are stored only on your device unless you choose to back them up or share them as described below.

Accounts

When you first open the App, it signs in anonymously with Firebase Authentication (Google). This creates a random identifier and does not collect your name or email. It lets you browse and download community templates without an account.

If you choose to link an email address (required to upload to the community library or use cloud backup), your email address is stored by Firebase Authentication and used only to verify your account and let you sign in.

Optional Cloud Features

  • Cloud Backup (off by default). When enabled, a compressed copy of your App data — including training data and any imported health workouts — is uploaded to Google Firebase (Cloud Storage and Firestore) under your account. Each new backup replaces the previous one. Only you can read it. You can turn backup off at any time, and you can delete your cloud backup from the Sync screen.
  • Community Library. Templates or themes you choose to upload, including their name, content and any images you attach, are published publicly so other users can download them. Health data is never included in community uploads.
  • Strava. If you connect Strava, the App uses Strava's sign-in to import your activities into your local training log. You can disconnect at any time in the App or in your Strava settings.
  • Wi-Fi Sync. Device-to-device sync sends your data directly between your own devices on your local network. It does not go through our servers.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only to scan QR codes for sharing templates and themes between devices. Photos you pick are used only as images in custom themes and are stored on your device (and in the community library only if you choose to upload that theme).

Analytics and Crash Reporting

We use Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage events (for example "a training cycle was created" or "a workout was completed") and basic device information, so we can improve the App. We do not send your weights, reps, workout content, health data, or email address to analytics.

We use Sentry to collect crash and error reports (stack traces and device/OS information) so we can fix bugs. Sentry is configured not to send personally identifiable information, and session replay is disabled. If you choose to send in-app feedback, the text and screenshot you submit are sent to Sentry.

We do not use advertising networks or advertising identifiers.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Data is shared only with the service providers that run the features above (Google Firebase, Sentry, and Strava if you connect it), and only to provide those features. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Your cloud backup stays until you delete it or replace it with a new backup.
  • To delete your account, linked email, cloud backup, or community uploads, contact us at the address below and we will delete them within 30 days.

Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Access to your cloud backup is restricted to your signed-in account.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with an email address, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can export your data at any time from the Sync screen, and you can contact us to exercise any of these rights.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
